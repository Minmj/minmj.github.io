Hi, I'm glad you wandered in.

What I do

I build small, well-tended things — web apps, tools, and the occasional library — and I write about the process along the way. I like projects that stay small on purpose: easier to understand, easier to keep alive.

How I got here

Like most of what's planted here, it started small: a project I wanted to exist, so I built it. That habit of starting minimal and letting things grow only as far as they need to is still how I work today.

What I'm into right now

Local-first apps that don't ask for an account, small tracked-in-git tools, and finding the smallest possible version of an idea before building it.
